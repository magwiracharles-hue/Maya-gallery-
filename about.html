The approach

Art made with feeling

Each artwork begins with curiosity: a colour combination, a remembered place, or a feeling that is difficult to put into words. Maya builds each piece through an intuitive process, letting the materials and the moment guide the way.

The result is art that feels personal, open to interpretation, and made to bring warmth and character into a space.

“Every colour holds a feeling. Every canvas tells a story.”
